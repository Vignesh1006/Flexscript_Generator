# 16 - Travel Distance Report

**Status:** PLANNED

## What it does
Exports every connection distance to Excel and totals actual travel along networks or A* after a run, drawn as a spaghetti diagram.

## Example
Moving packing 8 m cuts forklift travel by 14 km a week.

## Delivery
Script + draw overlay
